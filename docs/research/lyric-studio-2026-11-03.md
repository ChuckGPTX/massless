# Lyric Studio: research before implementation

Date: 3 November 2026

## Scope and method

This study covers **25 lyric-video examples plus 15 additional sources**. Video pages were opened in Chrome, sought to representative points, and visually inspected using screenshots and short excerpts. These are **sampled visual studies, not 25 complete end-to-end viewings**. Time anchors are approximate; some continued playing after seeking. A frame without words establishes a visual pause at that moment, not a claim about the entire video. No copyrighted footage, lyrics, illustrations or soundtracks are copied into Chucksi.

The additional sources include official product documentation, tutorials and primary research. Product claims about accuracy, speed or virality are not independent evidence. This study informs design; it does not prove that any output will trend.

## 15 additional sources

1. **[Canva lyric-video templates](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/templates/lyric/)** — Mood/genre choices, duplicated lyric pages and adjustable durations. Manual beat markers and paid automatic synchronization are different capabilities. Decision: reusable line blocks, not a new layout for every word.
2. **[CapCut AI lyric-video guide](https://www.capcut.com/resource/ai-lyric-video-maker)** — Upload, auto lyrics, language, animation, export. Recommends clear source audio, checking timing and phone readability. Decision: transcription is a draft with a visible correction stage, never a silent promise of perfection.
3. **[Adobe After Effects text-animation documentation](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/animating-text/text-animation/animating-text.html)** — Character, word and line selectors separate content from animation. Decision: text remains editable without rebuilding a visual preset.
4. **[Kapwing lyric-video maker](https://www.kapwing.com/tools/make/lyric-video)** — Manual and automatic lyric paths, typography, trimming, waveforms and export. Decision: pasted lyrics and timed subtitle imports must work even when transcription is unavailable.
5. **[School of Motion: kinetic typography, part 1](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/kinetic-typography-after-effects-part-1)** — Audio-first construction, waveform/event markers and organizing animation without duplicating audio. Decision: one audio clock drives preview; users can set cue boundaries while listening.
6. **[Kaiber lyric-video guide](https://feedback.kaiber.ai/help/articles/7460007-how-to-create-lyric-videos-in-kaiber)** — Correct lyrics, select a chorus, supply optional visual guidance and generate. Scene-integrated lettering is already a marketed feature. Decision: differentiate through editable timing, reusable styles and accessible export rather than calling that idea new.
7. **[Maxon: kinetic typography](https://www.maxon.net/en/solutions/kinetic-typography)** — Extrusion, camera, kerning and procedural text effects. Decision: preserve a future path to real 3D lettering; a 2D shadow must not be described as a 3D scene.
8. **[Motion Boutique: physics-based text animation](https://www.motionboutique.com/learn/physics-based-text-animation-organic-gradients)** — Letter shapes, anchors, collision complexity, repulsion and damping. Decision: dynamic entrances need a settled, readable hold. Physics everywhere is not an improvement.
9. **[Descript lyric-video maker](https://www.descript.com/tools/lyric-video-maker)** — Correctable transcript, timing adjustments, karaoke treatment, subtitle files and vertical safe areas. Decision: make editing and subtitle portability first-class capabilities.
10. **[VEED music-video maker](https://www.veed.io/create/music-video-maker)** — Audio timeline, trimming, artwork, filters, waveforms and resizing. Decision: preserve the uploaded song and support landscape, square and portrait without letterbox dependence.
11. **[Envato: lyric motion graphics in After Effects](https://elements.envato.com/learn/how-to-make-a-lyric-video-in-after-effects-motion-graphics)** — Rhythm, camera motion, motion blur and customization. Decision: restrained easing and transitions add polish; constant movement competes with reading.
12. **[Visual Lyrics, IUI 2026](https://www.davidlin.io/papers/visuallyrics.pdf)** — Primary research connecting word meaning, musical delivery and editable animation planning. Its study examined 20 tutorials and 50 videos; its system is a research prototype. Decision: support explicit chorus emphasis now; semantic, word-specific direction is a later validated capability, not a claim for this release.
13. **[Adobe Research: automated conversion of music videos into lyric videos](https://research.adobe.com/publication/automated-conversion-of-music-videos-into-lyric-videos/)** — Synchronization, readability, visual harmony and unified attention. Decision: use contrast and safe space so imagery and words do not fight for focus.
14. **[Fonts That Fit the Music, IEEE Access 2022](https://staff.aist.go.jp/m.goto/PAPER/IEEEACCESS202206haraguchi.pdf)** — Primary study of 3,494 points across 100 lyric videos and relationships among music, fonts and motion. Decision: provide genuinely different typographic directions. Correlation does not establish what will go viral.
15. **[Motionpilot: how to make a lyric video](https://www.motionpilot.app/blog/how-to-make-a-lyric-video)** — Clean repeated lyrics, phrase boundaries, timing before style, coherent fonts, readable holds, chorus emphasis and short hooks. Decision: support both complete songs and selected excerpts; correct lyrics before export.

## What we will build from the findings

- Upload MP3/WAV/M4A, preserve the original audio, and explicitly start transcription.
- Review transcription; edit every line, its start/end, and chorus emphasis. Singing and dense mixes can be misheard. Segment-derived or evenly spaced timing is an estimate, not forced alignment.
- Six distinct treatments: oversized color-block type; a paper journal; orbiting constellations; an audio-band landscape; dimensional type; and echo trails. Each enters, holds, then leaves. These are original implementations of established design techniques, not claims to have invented them.
- Landscape, portrait and square previews, custom artwork, a scrubber and waveform, and selected-clip or full-song export.
- Real MP4 with the original audio, plus editable SRT/LRC. No guaranteed-trending claims and no copyrighted reference assets shipped.
- A reduced-motion preference for the editor. The user chooses whether motion belongs in the exported video.

## Quality gates

Check import parsing, monotonic cue timing, lyric-free gaps, long words, non-Latin text, silence, cancellation, audio/video synchronization and exported file readability. Inspect the actual desktop and narrow-screen UI. Transcription success on spoken test audio does not demonstrate singing accuracy; that remains a user-review step until tested on consented songs.

## Later work requiring additional validation

Vocal separation and forced alignment; editable word-level timing; chorus detection; phrase-aware cuts; subject-aware text placement; real 3D type; semantic scene planning; background-video support; collaborative projects. These are not shipped by merely adding a label or a preset card.
