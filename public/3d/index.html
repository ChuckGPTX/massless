How it works

In your browser: Depth Anything V2 estimates depth on your device. Each sample of the still becomes a flat Gaussian placed along its camera ray, and a second layer built from the refilled background sits behind foreground objects. The result is the visible front of the scene in true 3D.

Full object: Connect a reconstruction service to create a complete object from one image, including its unseen sides. Your image is sent to the service you connect.

Splats render with Spark (MIT) on three.js.

Models & limits

Depth: Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0), in your browser. Splat viewer: Spark (MIT). Object reconstruction uses a connected service with its own terms. Local editing has no service charge.
