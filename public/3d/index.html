How it works

In your browser: Depth Anything V2 estimates depth on your device. Each sample of the still becomes a flat Gaussian placed along its camera ray, and a second layer built from the refilled background sits behind foreground objects. The result is the visible front of the scene in true 3D.

Idol on your GPU: Idol (speridlabs, Apache-2.0) predicts a complete 3D object as up to a million Gaussians from one image, including its unseen sides. It needs PyTorch and an NVIDIA GPU, so it runs on your machine through a small local server, and the browser only sends the picture and shows the result.

Splats render with Spark (MIT) on three.js.
