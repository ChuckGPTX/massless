How it works

Voiceover uses Kokoro-82M, an 82-million-parameter open-weight text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0), run through kokoro-js on WebGPU or WebAssembly. Text is split into sentences, spoken chunk by chunk and joined into one 24 kHz WAV.

Sound beds are built from noise, filters and envelopes in an OfflineAudioContext and exported as 48 kHz stereo WAV. Everything happens in this tab; nothing is uploaded.
