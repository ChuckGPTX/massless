How it works

A recipe, not a pile of code. Your sentence becomes a small JSON recipe: genre, look, names, tuning, a level seed and a tempo. Llama 3.3 70B writes it on Cloudflare's free allowance; if that's busy, a built-in writer reads your words and fills it in, offline.

Six hand-built templates. Runner, Stack (3D with three.js, or 2D), Shooter, Match 3, Maze and Eat & Grow, each tuned to stay fun for minutes, not seconds. The recipe only turns their dials, so every game works.

Playtested before you play. A bot plays every new game headless first: the maze must be solvable, every runner gap clearable with the jump arc, the match board must have moves, the shooter's first waves survivable, the stacker reachable to floor 30. If a seed fails, it rolls another.

Art and sound for $0. Characters and worlds are drawn in code. Turn on AI art and FLUX.1 schnell paints a background and sprites, and the backgrounds are removed in your browser. Sound effects are synthesized; the music is the Film page's score engine at the game's tempo.

Yours to keep. Share puts the recipe in the link. Download gives you one HTML file that plays offline, MIT licensed.
