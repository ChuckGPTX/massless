    How it works

    Words on the beat. Your brief becomes a beat sheet: a few words per beat, a block for each phrase, and the drop where the film turns. Llama 3.3 70B writes it on Cloudflare's free allowance, or a built-in template does, offline.

    Scored at an exact tempo. The music and the cut sounds are synthesized in your browser at the tempo you pick, so every cut lands on a beat by construction. No samples, no paid music model.

    Code moves it. Each frame is drawn by code: slams, whip pans, colour grounds, extruded 3D type, typed prompts, slot reels and your own stills. The export averages several sub-frames per frame across a 180° shutter for real motion blur, and WebCodecs writes an MP4 with sound.

    Models & limits

    Beat sheet: Llama 3.3 70B (open weights) on Cloudflare's free daily allowance, or a built-in template. Music and sound: synthesized in your browser. Motion and encoding: your browser. Cost: $0.
