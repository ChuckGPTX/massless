    What it replaces

    Paywalled studios charge credits per shot for this workflow and route it through closed video models. Massless rebuilds the two core features, a camera-and-lens shot designer and one-click camera moves, from their public behavior only. No proprietary code, prompts or assets were used.

    Inspired by the Open Source Higgsfield challenge. Source and the ComfyUI workflow are on GitHub under the MIT license.

    How it works

    1. Build the rig. Pick a camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, light and film stock. These become a precise prompt you can read and edit.
    2. Shoot. A free open-weight model renders the hero frame. You can also bring your own still.
    3. Move the camera. Your device estimates depth, turns the still into 3D, and flies a virtual camera through it: dolly zoom, crash zoom, arcs, rack focus and more.
    4. Record. Export an MP4 at 24 or 30 fps, or save a frame.

    Limits: people in the shot can't act, and big moves stretch the edges behind foreground objects. Keep intensity moderate for clean results.
