    How it works

    Edit: pdf.js (Apache-2.0) draws each page on your screen. Your highlights, text, ink, signatures and notes sit on top until you press Save, then pdf-lib (MIT) writes them into the PDF itself: highlights as see-through rectangles, text in Helvetica, ink as vector lines, signatures as images and notes as standard PDF comments.

    Convert: merge, split, rotate and image to PDF copy pages without re-rendering, so text stays sharp and searchable. PDF to images and Compress render pages with pdf.js; Compress rebuilds the file from JPEGs, so text stops being selectable. Zips are made with fflate (MIT).

    Ask: pulls the text out of the PDF in your browser and sends up to 12,000 characters of it, with your question, to Llama 3.3 70B on Cloudflare Workers AI. Edit and Convert never send anything anywhere.
