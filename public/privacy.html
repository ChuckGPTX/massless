Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Chucksi (chucksi.com) is a free, open-source set of creative tools. The short version: most tools run entirely in your browser, we keep as little as we can, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising. The code that does all of this is public on GitHub.

What stays on your device

Many tools (image cutouts, upscaling, editing, video cutting and more) run on your own device. The files you open in them are not uploaded to us. Some settings and your sign-in session are saved in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time.

Account information

You can use most of Chucksi without an account. If you create one, with email or by signing in with Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, X or Facebook, we receive your email address, and from those services your name and profile picture. We use this only to sign you in and keep your projects tied to you. We never get your password for those services, we never post on your behalf, and we do not ask for access to your files, contacts, friends or repositories.

Projects you save

When you save a project to the cloud, its files and settings are stored in our database and private storage so you can open them again. Only your account can read them. You can ask us at any time to delete a project or your whole account and everything in it.

Server-run AI tools

Some features send the text, audio or images you provide to a server to process. That input is used only to produce your result. It is not used to train models, and we do not keep it after the job is done unless you save the result. Our GPU provider holds a finished result only briefly, until it is delivered to you. In detail:

  • Creating images (Image, design workflows, website, slide and game art): your description is sent to Cloudflare. If you pick the Backup style, or when the free images are busy or used up, some tools send it to Pollinations instead. If you connect your own image workflow, it goes only to the address you enter.
  • Best quality images, text to video, Animate, Motion, Lip sync, Upscale video and Write a song: your description and any image, clip or audio you add are sent to RunPod GPU servers.
  • Fast text to video (members video): your description and any start frame are sent to OpenRouter and the video provider it uses.
  • Voiceover in six languages: your script is sent to Cloudflare, in short parts. English voices are made on your device.
  • Captions, transcripts and edit by text: only the sound of your file, downsampled to 16 kHz mono, is sent to Cloudflare, and only when you press Transcribe.
  • Ask a PDF: the text of the PDF open in Edit (up to 12,000 characters) and your question are sent to Cloudflare. The file itself is not uploaded.
  • Launch film scripts: your brief is sent to Cloudflare when “Write it with AI” is on.
  • Full 3D objects: your image is sent only to the object service address you enter.
  • Verify a signed PDF: only a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the file is sent.

Everything else, including editing, converting and signing PDFs, background removal, upscaling and erasing in the Image workroom, camera moves and effects, sound beds, extracting and mastering audio, slides, websites and games, runs on your own device.

Chucksi Sign

Documents sent with Chucksi Sign are encrypted in your browser before upload, and the key stays in the link, so we cannot read them. Encrypted envelopes are deleted automatically 30 days after sending if unsigned, and 90 days after completion.

Hosted websites

When you publish a site from the website builder, its files (pages, images, styles and sitemap) are uploaded and stored under your account so they can be served publicly at your v2web.site address. Anything you put on a published site is public. We keep the site's address, your account ID, file sizes and publish times, and count publishes per account and per hashed IP address to limit abuse. Unpublishing deletes the files right away. Visitors to hosted sites are served by Cloudflare like the rest of Chucksi; we add no tracking to hosted sites.

If you report a site, we store your report, a one-way hash of your IP address and, only if you give one, your contact email, to review the report and prevent abuse.

Abuse limits and security

To keep free tools free, we count daily use with a one-way hash of your IP address. These counters expire within about two days. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. Standard request logs are kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and debugging.

Service providers

Chucksi runs on these providers, each of which processes data only to deliver the service:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security, Turnstile and some AI models
  • Pollinations: the Backup image style (your description is sent to make the image)
  • Supabase: accounts, saved projects and file storage
  • RunPod: GPU servers for heavier video and audio jobs
  • OpenRouter: the free members video model (your prompt and any start frame are sent to make the clip)
  • Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, X and Facebook: only if you choose to sign in with them
  • GitHub and jsDelivr: delivery of the site's code and open models

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies.

Children

Chucksi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything stored with it, email chuckgptx@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, or tell us which service you signed in with. We delete your account, saved projects, published sites and files within 30 days and confirm by email. If you signed in with Google, GitHub, LinkedIn, X or Facebook, you can also remove Chucksi's access in that service's settings at any time.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions or deletion requests: chuckgptx@gmail.com.
