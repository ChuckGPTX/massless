    How it works

    Depth Anything V2 estimates depth on your device. Massless turns the still into a displaced mesh, cuts it at depth cliffs, fills a background layer behind foreground objects, and flies a virtual camera through it. Lens, film and grade are applied in a finishing pass, and WebCodecs encodes H.264 MP4 frame by frame.

    Limits: people in the frame can't act, and big moves stretch the background behind foreground objects. Keep intensity moderate for clean results.

    The $0 ledger

    Depth: Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0), in your browser. Camera, lens and film: Massless engine (MIT), in your browser. Encoding: your browser's H.264 encoder. Cost: $0.
