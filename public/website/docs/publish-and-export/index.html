From example to live website

A clear path through editing, review and publishing.

Start with the design

Choose a template in Website Studio or the Website template gallery. The website studio opens on chucksi.com. Sign in to your Chucksi account when you are ready to publish.

Make every example your own

Update the business name, contact details, navigation, page copy and images. Replace sample testimonials, dates, products and prices with verified information. Connect forms and payment links if needed; a template preview alone does not make those services operational.

Check the experience

Preview desktop, tablet and phone. Follow navigation links, test form destinations and check readable colours. Templates support motion, while respecting reduced-motion preferences.

Export or publish

You can edit and export without signing in. Hosted publishing requires a signed-in account in Website Studio and a review of your site. Public catalog and MCP calls do not bypass that requirement or create a hosted site.
